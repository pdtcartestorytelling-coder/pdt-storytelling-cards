The professional’s role

Do not use PdT for selection, hiring, promotion, exclusion, psychological assessment or scoring. Do not solicit sensitive information or private stories. Offer an alternative without penalty; respect data protection and non-discrimination.

Professional boundaries

PdT is proposed as a storytelling resource, not a diagnostic or psychometric test or a stand-alone treatment; it is not intended to treat or prevent disease. The final intended use requires approval. The theoretical references do not establish specific efficacy.
