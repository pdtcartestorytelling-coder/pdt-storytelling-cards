B2C legal guarantee

The seller is responsible for lack of conformity appearing within two years of delivery; defects appearing within one year are presumed to have existed at delivery unless incompatible with the nature of goods or defect. Consumers may choose free repair or replacement unless impossible or disproportionate, within a reasonable time and without significant inconvenience. Supplying a missing card may restore conformity. Proportionate price reduction or termination applies under Articles 135-bis onwards; termination is unavailable for minor defects. Necessary remedy costs are borne by the seller. The limitation period for defects not fraudulently concealed is 26 months from delivery, subject to statutory exceptions. No additional commercial guarantee is promised.
