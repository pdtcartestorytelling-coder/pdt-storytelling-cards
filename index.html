The Project

An evocative tool to give shape to inner stories

PDT Storytelling Cards was created as a supportive tool for individual, group and professional settings. Through evocative images, the cards help people recognize emotions, thoughts, memories, needs, resources and personal directions.

Each card becomes a starting point for opening a narrative, encouraging dialogue and supporting a deeper process of awareness.

How It Works

The cards can be used in different stages of a personal or professional journey

Emotional Exploration

To help people recognize, express and give words to what they feel through their connection with an image.

Personal Storytelling

To build narratives, connect experiences and give meaning to important moments in one’s personal story.

Resources and Goals

To identify possibilities, desires, directions and useful resources for growth and change.

Who It’s For

A tool designed for professionals who work with people

PDT Storytelling Cards is designed for psychologists, psychotherapists, counselors, coaches, educators, trainers and professionals working in helping relationships.

It can be used in clinical, educational, training, corporate and personal development contexts.
