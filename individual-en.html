PdT in individual work

Possible directions

  1. 01

    Make room for associations, metaphors and personal connections.

  2. 02

    Support storytelling about experiences, changes, resources and goals.

  3. 03

    Adapt questions to the remit and situation without imposing interpretations.

The professional’s role

The professional remains responsible for the setting and assesses distress. The person may decline to answer, choose another image or stop.

Professional boundaries

PdT is proposed as a storytelling resource, not a diagnostic or psychometric test or a stand-alone treatment; it is not intended to treat or prevent disease. The final intended use requires approval. The theoretical references do not establish specific efficacy.
