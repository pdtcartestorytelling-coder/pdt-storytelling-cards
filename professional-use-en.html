Professional use and limitations

Intended use of PdT Storytelling Cards.

Guidance on responsible use of photographic cards and guided questions.

UNAPPROVED DRAFT — proposed intended use, subject to review of manual, materials, evidence and classification before publication.

Proposed intended use

PdT Signature is a photographic storytelling resource intended to facilitate narration, reflection, dialogue and exploration of personal meanings in appropriate settings. It is not intended to diagnose, measure psychological traits, treat or prevent disease, or replace healthcare. Product classification must be assessed against actual intended purpose, instructions and promotional materials.

Professional competence and responsibility

Professionals assess suitability, agreed purpose, their competence and legally required qualifications. Reserved activities remain restricted to authorised professionals. Purchase does not confer professional qualifications. Cards do not provide automatic interpretations or predetermined clinical meanings.

Psychology and psychotherapy

Qualified professionals may integrate the kit as a narrative prompt in an appropriate setting without replacing assessment, clinical judgement or validated tools when required. Explain purpose and limitations, obtain informed consent where required, respect the choice not to answer or to stop. Assess suitability and distress; adapt or discontinue where appropriate. Do not use the kit as a stand-alone crisis intervention.

Groups, training and minors

Clarify purpose, participation, confidentiality arrangements and limits of sharing; do not require personal disclosures. Do not promise absolute confidentiality between participants. Use with minors or vulnerable people requires specific assessment of suitability, safety, consent/authorisations and applicable competence; no age range is validated here.

Human Resources

Proposed use: dialogue, communication and organisational reflection. Do not use image choices as scores, psychological profiles, health indicators, fitness criteria or a basis for hiring, promotion or exclusion decisions. Do not solicit sensitive or job-irrelevant information; avoid pressure to disclose private experiences. Observe employment, data protection and anti-discrimination law.

Evidence and limitations

Philosophical, psychological and cultural references explain the theoretical framework. They do not by themselves establish PdT-specific psychometric validity or clinical efficacy. No therapeutic outcome, diagnosis or measurable improvement is promised. [Review any product-specific studies and wording before approving scientific claims].

Confidentiality and online guide

Information emerging during activities must be handled by the responsible party under applicable rules, with minimisation, confidentiality and an appropriate legal basis. The website guide is informational, not clinical advice or an emergency service. Do not submit patient, candidate or employee data through forms or the guide. See the Privacy Policy.

Manufacturer responsibility and contact

These provisions do not exclude manufacturer or seller liability, product safety, statutory guarantees, wilful misconduct, gross negligence or other mandatory rights. Product enquiries: pdtstorytellingcards@gmail.com. Draft dated 26 November 2026.
