The PdT method

A narrative and relational perspective on human experience.

The PdT method arises from the encounter between clinical thought, image, narration and listening. Through evocative photographs, the work is oriented toward building personal and shared meanings.

Photography is understood not only as an image to observe, but as a narrative device capable of activating emotions, memories, representations and reflective processes.

The approach considers self-narration as a process that takes shape through dialogue, listening and shared experience.

Narrative phototherapy

Image, memory and meaning.

Photography as an activator

Images can facilitate access to emotional content, experiences and memories that may be difficult to reach through verbal language alone.

The narrative dimension

Telling stories means giving meaning to experience, integrating fragments of life and building new possibilities for self-understanding.

The relationship

Meaning emerges within an authentic, protected relationship oriented toward listening to subjectivity.
