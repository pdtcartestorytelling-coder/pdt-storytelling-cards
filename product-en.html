INSIDE THE KIT

Three resources, one complete tool.

Photographs provide an open-ended stimulus; the manual and activity sheets help shape an approach suited to the professional setting.

Two decks of 51 cards

102 original photographs, selected to offer a range of stimuli, moods and associations. The images have no predetermined meaning: each person shapes their own story.

Manual and activity sheets

The 60-page theoretical and practical manual introduces the methodology and its applications. It incorporates 22 guided activity sheets: 8 for individual work, 6 for groups and 8 for human resources.

The magnetic box

A single box designed to hold both decks and the manual. Italian design and planned Made in Italy production, with final blue and gold artwork.

THE METHOD

From images to stories.

    Observe

    Choose a photograph and allow associations to emerge.

  2. 02

    Share

    Make space for the person's words and meanings.

  3. 03

    Explore

    Use guided questions suited to the setting.
