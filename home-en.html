Storytelling Cards

The project

An evocative tool to give shape to personal stories

The project was created to provide professionals with an operational storytelling tool that supports the knowledge, processing and integration of emotions, thoughts, experiences and personal representations.

Its ultimate aim is to activate new intra- and interpersonal resources towards psychophysical well-being.

The product

Photographic cards for emotional exploration and self-narration

PdT is designed to support self-awareness and the development of personal resources through evocative images and dedicated operational tools.

The PdT bundle includes two decks of 51 cards each, a theoretical and operational manual, and guide sheets with questions divided by themes and professional contexts.

The cards

The images have been carefully created and selected. They are balanced across evocative themes, from disturbing material to safer and more protective content.

The manual

The theoretical-operational manual guides professionals step by step in understanding the PdT method and its theoretical-scientific connections.

The guide sheets

The sheets offer operational questions divided by themes and contexts, supporting the use of the product in different professional settings.

How it works

A tool to be used within a protected professional context

The cards should be administered in a professional setting, protected in time and method according to the mandate shared between professional and client.

Emotional exploration

To help people perceive, recognize, name and share what they feel in connection with the image.

Personal and group storytelling

To build narratives, connect experiences and give meaning to more complex life passages.

Resources and goals

To identify new possibilities, desires, directions and resources useful for growth and change.

Who it is for

For professionals in care, education and human development

PdT is intended for healthcare professionals, psychologists, psychotherapists, educators, pedagogists, corporate counselors and HR professionals.
