The project

An evocative tool to know, process and integrate.

PdT was created to offer professionals an operational storytelling tool that can accompany emotions, thoughts, experiences and personal representations.

Images and guide questions support meaning-making and the activation of new intra- and interpersonal resources toward psychophysical well-being.

The product

Two decks, one manual and operational guides.

The PdT bundle includes two decks of 51 photographic cards each, a 47-page theoretical-operational manual and guide sheets organised by themes and professional contexts.

01

The cards

Images carefully created and selected, balanced between unsettling material and safer, more protective content.

02

The manual

A theoretical-operational path that guides professionals through the method and its theoretical and scientific connections.

03

The guide sheets

Questions organised by themes, such as identity and emotions, and by individual, group and human-resources contexts.

How it works

Professional, protected and shared use.

The cards are designed for use in a professional context protected in the timing and methods of the declared and shared mandate.

01

Emotional exploration

To perceive, recognise, name and share what emerges in connection with the image.

02

Personal and group storytelling

To build narratives, connect experiences and give meaning to more complex life transitions.

03

Resources and goals

To identify new possibilities, desires, directions and resources useful for growth and change.
