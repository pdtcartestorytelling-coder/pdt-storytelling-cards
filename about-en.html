The origins of the project

From interdisciplinary dialogue to PdT.

The collaboration developed over time through shared interests in psychoanalysis, cinema and the arts. Exchanging ideas and experiences led to a project centred on subjectivity, listening and storytelling.

Meet the founders

Co-founder · Photography and philosophy

Roberto Antoniello

He holds a master’s degree in analytic philosophy and philosophy of mind, combining theoretical research with a multidisciplinary artistic and cultural path.

A photographer interested in abstraction and uncanny atmospheres, he explores perception, emotion and interpretation. He also works in editorial writing and has an interest in European auteur cinema.

Co-founder · Clinical psychology

Silvia Dierico

A clinical psychologist and psychoanalytic psychotherapist. She works with adolescents and adults.

The project’s professional profile describes coordination of a multidisciplinary team in a Family Counselling Centre, community interventions, school counselling and health-promotion project skills.
