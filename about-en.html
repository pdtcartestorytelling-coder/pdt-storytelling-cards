About us

A human and professional encounter.

PdT - Parlami di Te was born from the collaboration between Roberto Antoniello and Silvia Dierico and from a shared vision of human experience as a space for listening, storytelling and self-discovery.

Our story

More than twelve years of dialogue and shared design.

The collaboration between Roberto Antoniello and Silvia Dierico began with a human and professional encounter that has lasted for more than twelve years.

Over time, an initial consulting path became an ongoing and authentic dialogue nourished by shared interests in psychoanalysis, cinema and the arts.

Through a constant exchange of ideas, reflections and experiences, a common vision emerged, attentive to the emotional, narrative and relational dimensions of human experience.

From this harmony, PdT - Parlami di Te took shape as a space for encounter, listening, storytelling and self-discovery.

The co-founders

Two distinct paths, one shared vision.

Co-founder · Photography & philosophy

Roberto Antoniello

Roberto Antoniello holds a master’s degree in analytic philosophy and philosophy of mind, combining theoretical research with a multidisciplinary artistic and cultural path.

As a photographer with a strong sensitivity for abstraction and uncanny atmospheres, he explores the boundaries between perception, emotion and interpretation.

He also works in editorial writing and cultivates an interest in the history of European auteur cinema, with attention to its aesthetic and philosophical dimensions.

Co-founder · Clinical psychology

Silvia Dierico

Silvia Dierico is a clinical psychologist and intersubjective psychotherapist working with adolescents and adults through paths of growth, awareness and care.

She coordinates a multidisciplinary team within a Family Counselling Centre, integrating clinical, relational and design skills.

She develops community interventions aimed at psychological well-being and specialises in school counselling and health-promotion projects.

PdT - Parlami di Te

Images, clinical thought and artistic sensitivity.

The project integrates different skills to create a professional tool attentive to subjectivity, relationship and the narrative construction of experience.
