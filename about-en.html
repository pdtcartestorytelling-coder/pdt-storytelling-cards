About us

A human and professional encounter

PdT - Parlami di Te was born from the collaboration between Roberto Antoniello and Silvia Dierico, and from a shared vision of human experience as a space for listening, storytelling and self-discovery.

The collaboration

Twelve years of dialogue, research and shared vision

The collaboration between Roberto Antoniello and Silvia Dierico began from a human and professional encounter that has lasted for more than twelve years.

Over time, an initial consulting path became an ongoing and authentic dialogue, nourished by shared interests in psychoanalysis, cinema and the arts.

Through a constant exchange of ideas, reflections and experiences, a common vision emerged, attentive to the emotional, narrative and relational dimensions of human experience.

From this harmony, the PdT - Parlami di Te project took shape as a space for encounter, listening, storytelling and self-discovery.

Founders

Two distinct paths, one shared vision

RA

Roberto Antoniello

Roberto Antoniello holds a master’s degree in analytic philosophy and philosophy of mind, combining theoretical research with a multidisciplinary artistic and cultural path.

As a photographer with a strong sensitivity for abstraction and uncanny atmospheres, he explores the boundaries between perception, emotion and interpretation.

SD

Silvia Dierico

Silvia Dierico is a clinical psychologist and intersubjective psychotherapist working with adolescents and adults in paths of growth, awareness and care.

She coordinates a multidisciplinary team within a Family Counseling Center and develops community interventions aimed at psychological well-being.

PdT - Parlami di Te

A space for encounter, listening and narration

PdT Storytelling Cards comes from the integration of image, clinical thought, artistic sensitivity and attention to relationship.
