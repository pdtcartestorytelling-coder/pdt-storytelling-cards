<h2>2. Data, purposes and legal bases</h2><h3>Browsing</h3><p>Hosting receives technical data necessary to deliver pages, including IP addresses. GitHub Pages states that IP addresses are logged for security. For processing attributable to the controller, the proposed basis is the legitimate interest in operating and securing the website (Article 6(1)(f)); providers' independent processing is explained in their notices.</p>
<h3>Contact and Signature pre-order enquiries</h3><p>The contact form requires full name, email and message. The Pre Order form requires full name, email, indicative number of sets (initially one) and acknowledgement of this notice; it includes optional delivery city/country and message and the fixed Signature product. Ordinary email metadata may also be received. Availability enquiry links prepare a draft requesting availability, quantity, final price and delivery details.</p><p>We respond to enquiries, provide Signature information and manage requested pre-contractual steps. Article 6(1)(b) applies to requested pre-contractual steps; legitimate interests in handling general correspondence apply under Article 6(1)(f). Applicable legal duties rely on Article 6(1)(c). Acknowledging this notice is not consent to processing necessary to respond.</p><p>Forms prepare a draft in your email application. In normal operation no message is automatically sent and the site does not store submissions in a database. The controller receives the contents when you send the email. An enquiry is not an order, binding reservation or payment. Required fields are necessary to use the respective form; without contact details we cannot reply. Optional details may be omitted. Do not include health information or identifiable or confidential information about patients, clients, employees or third parties.</p>
<h3>Updates and marketing</h3><p>This website configuration does not collect new promotional opt-ins. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to a newsletter. Any historical consents will not be used for promotional messages until their validity, purposes and withdrawal procedure have been verified. You may withdraw consent or object to direct marketing by contacting the privacy contact, without affecting previous lawful processing. A future newsletter is planned, but it is not active in this configuration. Before activation, notices, separate consent, consent evidence and withdrawal procedures will be updated or established.</p>
<h3>Information guide</h3><p>The guide uses predefined answers processed in the browser. The reviewed code does not transmit questions to an external AI service or persistently save them. Do not enter personal or health information. It is not clinical advice.</p>
