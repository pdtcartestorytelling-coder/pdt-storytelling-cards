WHO IT'S FOR

Different perspectives. One shared language: images.

From one-to-one settings to organisations, PdT provides structured resources to make room for personal experiences and interpretations.

01

One-to-one work

Photographs can offer a starting point to explore experiences, memories and meanings, at a pace suited to the person and their context.

02

Group settings

A shared image can open a conversation, with different perspectives creating opportunities to listen and exchange ideas.

03

Human Resources

Visual prompts and guided questions to integrate into reflection, dialogue and professional development activities.

Created at the meeting point of photography, storytelling and psychological practice.

DIGITAL · LICENCE

Private access to digital resources.

We are designing a unique licence system: a QR code would open the activation page, while a private code inside the package would link a product to a user account. Documents would be accessible only after verifying the licence.

01

Activation

In the future, link the unique product code to your account.

02

Edition access

Low and Mid provide the relevant digital resources; Top also includes printed copies.

03

Protection

Documents would not be public. Authentication limits sharing but cannot prevent screenshots or copies.

This system is only planned: accounts, codes and protected documents are not available yet.
