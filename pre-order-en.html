WHO IT'S FOR

Different perspectives. One shared language: images.

From one-to-one settings to organisations, PdT provides structured resources to make room for personal experiences and interpretations.

01

One-to-one work

Photographs can offer a starting point to explore experiences, memories and meanings, at a pace suited to the person and their context.

02

Group settings

A shared image can open a conversation, with different perspectives creating opportunities to listen and exchange ideas.

03

Human Resources

Visual prompts and guided questions to integrate into reflection, dialogue and professional development activities.

Created at the meeting point of photography, storytelling and psychological practice.

Meet the creators ↗

THE COLLECTION

Everything in one set to begin a narrative journey.

Photography, guiding questions and a theoretical-practical framework come together in one presentation box. Each element plays its own part.

01

102 photographic cards

Two decks of 51 photographs each, offering varied prompts for reflection and storytelling.

02

22 guided activity sheets

8 for individual work, 6 for groups and 8 for Human Resources settings.

03

Theoretical and practical manual

60 pages outlining the method, its theoretical background and ways to work with the materials.

04

Magnetic presentation box

A rigid case designed to organise and protect the complete set.

HOW IT WORKS

From an image to a story, step by step.

One possible approach: a photograph opens the conversation and guiding questions help explore what emerges.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Begin with a photograph and the associations it inspires.

  2. 02

    Tell a story

    Make space for words, memories and personal interpretations.

  3. 03

    Explore further

    The activity sheets offer prompts to deepen the conversation.
